# Creative direction — 12 October 2026

Visitor emotion: precision, clarity and confidence. Primary task: put product and service requirements into a qualified quotation enquiry.

## Three opening concepts

1. **Editorial glassware study — chosen.** An original studio composition of transparent glassware and violet liquid takes over the canvas behind a restrained headline. Measurement rails establish scientific precision. The image scales subtly with scroll, with a calm transition into real category photography. Strong brand relevance, a readable opening and a clear catalogue CTA. Mobile gives the subject its own scene before copy and actions; tablet follows the same principle. Reduced motion preserves the settled composition. Generated imagery is expressly illustrative.
2. **Full laboratory documentary scene.** Authentic supporting laboratory imagery establishes the environment immediately, but lacks a client-specific subject and risks feeling like a stock-photo banner. Useful for category storytelling, rather than the lead scene.
3. **Scientific equipment turntable.** A real 3D product could reveal components and specifications through interaction. No verified inventory, models or product specifications are supplied, so this would introduce unsupported product detail. Reserved for a later confirmed catalogue.

Prominent methods: ST-02 living subject, ST-04 practical discovery and quote utility, ST-08 connected conversion. The laboratory/workplace scene provides a deliberate change of environment with coordinated imagery, copy and destinations. No exclusivity or award claims are made.

Smartview’s retrieved source provided the reference for corporate clarity, real subject matter and prominent contact actions. Purple Sense uses its own visual composition, identity, page structure, palette and procurement flow.

UI/UX Pro Max’s first broad search was a poor fit (neumorphism). The narrower B2B procurement query supported accessible controls, restrained hierarchy and direct conversion. Its default palette, unverified proof badges and SaaS layout were not adopted; the supplied brief governs the brand.

Typography: Manrope headings, DM Sans body. Plum #38165C, violet #7652A0, lavender #E9E2F1, ivory #F8F8F7 and charcoal #25312E. Motion is CSS plus a passive, requestAnimationFrame-bound hero progression. No heavy 3D runtime is needed for this phase.
