## Direction selection

1. **Stepped dark composition, around 3–4 seconds:** creates a cinematic centre, but a dark treatment and stepped tiers would compete with the brief’s clear ivory scientific identity.
2. **Product-led editorial zones, around 4–5 seconds:** strong hierarchy and product focus, but many small supporting panels would fragment the company’s two connected supply/service worlds.
3. **Organic multi-region composition, around 6–7 seconds — selected:** shaped, unequal areas connect title, a dominant subject and an offset secondary image. This gives the existing glassware visual a stronger composition and connects it to workplace care without copying the coffee subject, wording or palette.
